# Announcement — The Metabolic Child (v3, 2026)

## Substack post

Most children who die of an inborn error of metabolism die before anyone says the word "metabolic". The baby who fed well for three days and then became sleepy is treated for sepsis, and nobody sends the ammonia. The toddler who is "always drowsy after a tummy bug" is sent home with ORS.

I have rebuilt *The Metabolic Child* around the first hour. It covers the five bedside results that point to a disease family, the glucose drip that actually stops catabolism, the critical sample that can end years of uncertainty, and the phone call to the metabolic centre that has to happen before the diagnosis is known. The later units go through muscle, liver, seizures, movement and development, then genomics, newborn screening, and what the National Policy for Rare Diseases can and cannot promise a family.

It has 20 units, with a role note in every unit for students, PG residents and faculty. It is free and works offline on a phone.

→ https://vikramsakaleshpurkumar-byte.github.io/Vikkypaedia-Metabolic-Child-MOOC/
