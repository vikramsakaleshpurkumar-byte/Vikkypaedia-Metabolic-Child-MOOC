# The Metabolic Child

Facilitator guide • Educational edition 2.2 • September 2026

## What this module contains

Twenty-one complete text lessons, 105 depth-tagged teaching sections, four fictional patient journeys with three decisions each, a three-question baseline, five-question evaluation in every room and a role-specific final practice set. Rooms 1–10 teach the core clinical approach. Rooms 11–21 form the Clinical Systems Masterclass, informed by the supplied 2018 *Pediatric Clinics of North America* IEM review. The course contains 138 authored questions overall. The final set contains six questions for UGs, 12 for PGs and 18 for faculty. Every question includes explanatory feedback and confidence recording. The course also contains a faculty pathway-writing exercise, personal reflections, eight printable study aids and a linked evidence library.

This is an interactive educational edition. It has not undergone independent metabolic-specialist approval, learner-pilot validation or accreditation. It has no recorded video/audio lectures, live teaching, cloud learner accounts or institutional gradebook. Browser-local progress and downloadable records support individual study. It is suitable for review and piloting before wider curricular deployment.

## The promise and its limits

MBBS learners should recognise concern, organise basic findings, identify immediate priorities and communicate within supervised practice. Paediatric residents should reason through the differential, select useful investigations and coordinate care with specialists. Faculty should justify decisions, teach the reasoning and construct a workable local pathway.

The course deliberately avoids individual prescriptions, drug-dose calculators and generic therapeutic diets. Actual treatment and specimen procedures need current clinical protocols and the receiving laboratory's SOPs. Online practice performance is not evidence of independent bedside competence.

## Customise the path

Choose UG, PG or Faculty in the top bar. The role changes the depth assigned to each section. Choose Must Know, Must + Nice to Know, or All three depths. Essential content is always retained; every learner may explore deeper material. Faculty implementation exercises become essential for the faculty path.

Must Know means required for the chosen role. Nice to Know extends reasoning. Good to Know supports advanced exploration. These are educational design decisions, not official NMC topic classifications. Exact NMC competency-code mapping remains to be completed from authoritative documents.

## Suggested teaching sequence

1. Ask learners to answer the three baseline questions before opening lessons. They should choose their confidence before feedback.
2. Use rooms 1–5 for recognition, the sick neonate, patterns, initial priorities and specimens.
3. Work through Aarav and Meera. Ask learners to explain the decision before selecting it.
4. Use rooms 6–10 for confirmation, later presentations, continuing care, screening/counselling and Indian pathways.
5. Use rooms 11–21 as the Clinical Systems Masterclass: overview, acidosis, hyperammonaemia, hypoglycaemia, cognitive impairment, seizures, movement disorders, myopathy/FAOD, hepatopathy, lysosomal/peroxisomal disease and CDG–mitochondrial overlap.
6. Work through Kabir and Sana, then complete final practice for the chosen role.
7. Faculty draft a local pathway, self-review it against the rubric, and discuss it with clinical, laboratory, dietetic and referral teams.
8. Invite a delayed return to the cases and a new supervised discussion. The current module does not schedule reminders or provide a second independently validated examination bank.

The room estimates are planning estimates, not measured completion times. Pilot actual time by role and depth. Allow extra time for case discussion, reflections and the faculty task.

## Assessment interpretation

First-attempt correctness is retained. A corrected answer is labelled as current practice after feedback and does not overwrite the first attempt. An incorrect case decision must be revisited before the next stage opens. This is deliberate practice with feedback; it is not a high-stakes examination.

A room is complete when its required explanations have been self-checked and all five evaluation questions have been corrected. Final practice is complete when all questions in the selected role's set have been corrected. There is no validated pass score, professional certificate or automatic claim of competence. The learning record separates self-report, first attempts and corrected practice.

Review confidently wrong answers with learners. Ask what led to the error and what observation would change their mind. The baseline and final sets differ, so a numerical difference is not a validated estimate of learning gain.

## Indian context and local additions

The cases include district and medical-college settings, limited testing, sample transport, practical referral and family access. Add current local test availability, actual receiving-laboratory SOPs, referral contacts and a metabolic dietitian's regional nutrition examples after clinical verification. Do not put patient-identifying material in course reflections.

Policy figures are dated. CoE designation is not a guarantee of a specific clinical service, treatment approval or funding. Confirm the current operative policy and the actual receiving service before using a local directory in practice.

## Review before wider use

Ask a metabolic clinician and neonatal/paediatric emergency clinician to review clinical statements, fictional results and decision feedback. Ask the receiving laboratory to review the specimen teaching and a metabolic dietitian/genetic counsellor to review relevant content. Invite UG, PG and faculty learners to test clarity, difficulty, completion time, mobile use and confidence calibration.

Record reviewer comments, resulting changes, a content owner and review date. Review new clinical and policy guidance when it changes rather than relying on website upload dates. Source records distinguish full-text access, abstracts, summaries and listings. Links and original explanations are provided; no claim is made to have acquired restricted textbook chapters or provider recordings.

## Offline and personal records

The supplied HTML file contains the complete text course and interactions without external libraries. Open it in a modern browser. Linked sources need internet access. Browser storage behaviour varies, particularly for local files and private browsing. Download reflections and pathway drafts before moving browsers or devices. The selected study guide can be saved as HTML and printed or saved as PDF through the browser.

The public GitHub Pages edition supports open learner access. Institutional LMS integration, SCORM packaging, account-based progress and accredited assessment require a separate distribution and learning-management decision.
