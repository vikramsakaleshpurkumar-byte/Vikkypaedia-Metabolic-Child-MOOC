# Approach to a child with an inborn error of metabolism

Source strategy and course blueprint • 11 September 2026

The course should leave learners able to explain their next clinical decision and its reason. Recognition, initial priorities, investigation, interpretation, referral and family communication form its backbone. A small set of disease examples makes those decisions memorable.

This document and its companion workbook are the evidence-selection and production blueprint. The completed teaching lessons, validated clinical job aids and learner testing come next.

## The learner promise

| Learner | Demonstrable outcome |
|---|---|
| UG: MBBS learner | In a case or supervised setting, recognise possible IEM, interpret basic patterns, identify urgent priorities and communicate an appropriate referral. |
| PG: paediatric resident | Prioritise the differential, select and interpret investigations, plan initial care with specialist support, and explain follow-up and counselling. |
| Faculty: general paediatric or medical teacher | Justify decisions, teach clinical reasoning, assess learners and design a feasible local clinical, laboratory and referral pathway. |

Completion of an online course demonstrates performance in its assessments. Independent bedside competence needs supervised workplace assessment.

## Customisation that preserves essential learning

Retain the requested labels, with clear subtitles: **Must Know — required for my role; Nice to Know — extend my reasoning; Good to Know — explore advanced practice.** Role and depth are separate choices. The workbook assigns each of 34 outcomes a proposed label for each role. These are design recommendations, not official NMC classifications.

For example, recognising urgent hyperammonaemia belongs in every role's required path. Detailed amino-acid/acylcarnitine/organic-acid report interpretation is enrichment for a UG and required learning for a paediatric PG. Critiquing a new gene therapy remains optional advanced learning. An experienced learner may demonstrate an existing skill on an entry case and skip its explanation, but should still demonstrate the required safety decisions.

## Ten rooms and four recurring families

The proposed rooms are: suspect IEM; the sick neonate; read the metabolic pattern; the first hour; send the right sample; phenotype to diagnosis; the child beyond the NICU; living with a diagnosis; screening and the next pregnancy; and IEM care in India.

Use four original fictional families: a deteriorating neonate, a toddler with fasting-associated illness, a child with regression, and a screen-positive infant. Reveal clinical data, investigations, diagnosis, illness plans, schooling, family questions and access problems at different points. Include an unfamiliar exit case so learners must transfer their reasoning.

## The Indian layer is part of every room

Obtain real receiving-laboratory SOPs, sample-transport instructions and clinically reviewed teaching reports. Collect actual local test availability and turnaround information rather than inventing a national price list. Include district-hospital and medical-college versions of the same decision. Ask an Indian metabolic dietitian to create regional food and growth-monitoring examples, and caregivers to review the clarity and practicality of family materials.

The [NMC rules index](https://www.nmc.org.in/rules-regulations-nmc/) identifies the revised CBME curriculum dated 12 September 2024. The earlier August 2024 version was withdrawn. Exact competency codes should be confirmed from the correct authoritative files before claiming formal alignment; this blueprint does not invent codes or imply NMC endorsement.

The [DBT UMMID document](https://dbtindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/Advt_UMMID_10062020_1.pdf) provides genetics, screening and counselling programme context. It is a programme document, not a verified current emergency referral directory.

The [13 March 2026 PIB statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2239572) reports support up to ₹50 lakh for 63 identified rare diseases through 15 designated CoEs. The [later MoHFW policy listing](https://www.mohfw-dohfw.gov.in/documents/acts-and-policies) includes a July 2026 notice adding AIIMS Nagpur. Therefore, date the March figures and maintain the referral information separately. Do not promise individual eligibility or payment, and do not equate CoE designation with verified emergency metabolic capacity.

## Materials we must create or acquire

1. Full IJPP articles and licensed textbook chapters; review selected ACMG sheets and other protocols individually.
2. Four branching cases and annotated laboratory reports with validated values, units and context.
3. A clinical-review-approved first-hour card, specimen checklist, pattern worksheet and referral handover.
4. Local referral and laboratory routes, with an owner and last-checked date.
5. Dietitian-authored nutrition cases and plain-language, locally reviewed family information.
6. Role-specific questions, short-answer tasks, feedback explanations and faculty assessment rubrics.
7. Short videos with equivalent readable lessons, captions and printable low-bandwidth alternatives. Target [WCAG 2.2 AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and test the delivered course.

Public access and reuse permission are different. IJPP's issue notice allows reproduction with acknowledgment; preserve that attribution and check third-party material separately. Other sources have their own terms. Write original cases and explanations, and verify permission before reproducing figures, slides or recordings. No permissions or collaborator requests have been sent.

## Show that learners can use what they learned

Use an entry case, repeated decision questions with explanations, an unfamiliar exit case and delayed retrieval. Ask learners to rate confidence before seeing feedback, then compare confidence with correctness. Reassess missed critical actions through another case instead of letting extra factual marks conceal them. Faculty should produce and defend a local pathway and demonstrate how they would teach it.

A [randomised clinical-reasoning study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27295475/) supports repeated key-feature questions, while a [residency trial of spaced-learning alerts](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33389233/) did not show the planned meaningful examination advantage. These support careful design and local evaluation, not a guarantee of effectiveness. Expert reviewers should set passing standards, and a pilot with all three learner groups should test difficulty, clarity, retention and decision performance.

The first production unit should combine suspicion, the sick child, first-hour decisions and essential samples. The companion workbook supplies its learning outcomes, candidate sources, assessment tasks and production assets, as well as the expansion path for the whole course.
